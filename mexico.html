Land of ancestral history, megadiversity, and rich culture

📍 General Information

  • Capital: Mexico City (CDMX)
  • Official Language: Spanish + 68 indigenous languages
  • Currency: Mexican Peso (MXN)
  • Organization: 31 States and 1 CDMX

🌤️ Climate & Geography

Mexico boasts one of the most diverse geographies in the world: from turquoise beaches in the Caribbean and the Pacific to arid desert landscapes in the north, tropical jungles in the south, and snow-capped volcanoes in the central region.

🎭 Vibrant Culture & Traditions

Day of the Dead

A globally renowned celebration designated as UNESCO Cultural Heritage that honors ancestors with vibrant altars, marigold flowers, sugar skulls, and colorful festivities.

Mariachi & Music

The iconic sound of Mexico. Mariachi music is an essential element of Mexican identity, accompanying life's most significant celebrations.

Popular Handcrafts

From intricate Talavera pottery and brightly painted alebrijes to handcrafted textiles created by indigenous communities throughout the country.

🌮 Gastronomy as Intangible Cultural Heritage

Traditional Mexican cuisine was declared Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity by UNESCO. It is rooted in ancestral ingredients like corn, chilies, beans, tomatoes, avocado, and cacao, harmoniously blended with colonial and modern culinary techniques.

🚌 Tourist Transportation & Travel Packages

🚇 Getting Around Mexico

  • Domestic Flights: Fast connections between key cities and regions via airlines like Volaris, VivaAerobus, and Aeroméxico.
  • Long-Distance Buses: Luxury intercity coaches (ADO, ETN) equipped with high-comfort reclining seats and Wi-Fi.
  • Urban Mobility: Safe and widely available rideshare apps (Uber, Didi) in major urban centers.
  • Scenic Trains: Experience the Maya Train across the Yucatán Peninsula or El Chepe through the Copper Canyon.
